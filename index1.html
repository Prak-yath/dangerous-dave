DANGEROUS DAVE

— 2026 REDUX —
A from-scratch homage & enhancement — WASD + Arrows supported

CONTROLS

WASD or ◀▲▼▶ — move / climb / duck
SPACE / W / ▲ — jump (hold with jetpack to fly)
F / J — fire gun (once picked up)
P / ESC — pause
R — restart from last checkpoint

OBJECTIVE

Reach the door to clear the level — it's never locked. Trophies are optional: each one adds score and builds a combo multiplier, and grabbing every trophy in a level pays out a bonus, but you never have to collect them to finish. Avoid spikes, lava and monsters, ride the jetpack once found, and touch checkpoint flags to save your progress in the level.

SETTINGS

DIFFICULTY

PITFALLS OF THE 1988 ORIGINAL → FIXED

1. Keyboard lock-in. Only arrow keys worked. → Full WASD + Arrows + remappable-feel dual scheme + on-screen touch pad for mobile.

2. Stiff, floaty movement. No acceleration/friction curve made platforming feel unfair. → Proper accel/decel physics, coyote-time (jump just after leaving a ledge) and jump-buffering (press jump slightly early).

3. One hit, one death, back to the whole level. → Multiple lives, mid-level checkpoint flags, and hit invulnerability frames with visual feedback.

4. Pixel-perfect, unforgiving hitboxes on spikes/enemies. → Hitboxes are intentionally shrunk from the sprite so near-misses feel fair.

5. No pause, no settings, no volume control. → Full pause menu, music/SFX sliders, mute, and a dedicated settings screen.

6. Zero accessibility options. → High-contrast mode, reduce-motion toggle, screen-shake toggle, large-target touch controls.

7. Fixed low resolution, not responsive. → Canvas scales fluidly from phone to desktop, crisp on every size.

8. No mobile support at all. → Native on-screen D-pad, jump and fire buttons auto-detected on touch devices.

9. No progress saving. → Level unlocks, best times and high scores persist locally between sessions.

10. Trophies were a mandatory gate — miss one and the door stays locked, forcing full backtracking. → Trophies are now optional bonus score/combo pickups; the door is always open, so the level is always finishable.

11. Obscure, undocumented warp zones. → Bonus routes are sign-posted with a visible glow instead of pure trivia/memorization.

12. Repetitive, unreadable enemy patterns. → Enemies telegraph movement (patrol bars / sine-wave bats) so patterns can be learned fairly.

13. No level overview. → Level-select map with stars, best time and lock state.

14. No game-feel/juice — flat hits, no particles. → Screen shake, hit-flash, particle bursts, glowing jetpack trail, camera easing.

15. Jetpack fuel was invisible/unclear. → Always-visible fuel gauge that flashes red near empty.

16. No scoring incentive beyond points. → Combo multiplier for chaining trophies without taking damage.

17. No end-of-level feedback. → Level-complete recap: time, deaths, medal (bronze/silver/gold) vs. a par time.

18. Same difficulty for everyone. → Easy / Normal / Hardcore modes changing lives, enemy speed and checkpoint availability.

19. Could get hit while "paused" in the original engine class of games. → Pausing fully freezes simulation, input and timers.

20. Nothing to look at — flat EGA color blocks. → Neon cave palette, parallax depth fog, animated lighting, optional CRT filter for nostalgia.

21. High-speed tunneling through thin platforms at speed. → Swept, order-independent collision resolution per axis.

22. Gravity too strong, jump arcs too short — some platforms felt unreachable and falls felt sudden. → Tuned gravity down, capped fall speed, and raised jump height so every platform is reachable with room to react.
